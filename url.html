Problem statement

Title: URL rewriting: put two ids in the next-page link

When cookies are off, identity can travel in the URL. Url1 builds a link. Url2 reads both numbers.

What you must do:

  1. GET form: enter 054 and 055. Submit to Url1.
  2. Url1 prints a next page link that already contains both ids.
  3. Click it. Url2 prints s_id1=054 and s_id2=055. Address bar shows both ids.

Sample output (same HTML Tomcat prints at http://localhost:8080/basic-servlets/...):

next page
