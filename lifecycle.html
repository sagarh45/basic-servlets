Small project: WatchTube (like YouTube)

One HTML page. Two Java classes are allowed. Refresh the video many times. Then read the channel stamp.

ICA journal Run project Source

What this project does

WatchTube opens a video once (init). Every click or refresh is one more view (service). The channel name is shared for the whole site (ServletContext). This video’s author is private (ServletConfig). Those two values come from web.xml, not from the form.

  1. Open the video (GET) at least five times. init stays 1. service grows.
  2. Leave your name as a viewer (POST).
  3. Open the channel stamp. Copy channel name (context) and author (config).
