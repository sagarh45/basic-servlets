Title: Sample registration form (POST only)

This is a full student registration form. It is only POST so name, password and other fields stay out of the URL.

What you must do:

  1. Fill the form (name, roll, email, mobile and the other fields).
  2. Submit. The result page must show request.getMethod() = POST and a table of every field.
  3. Confirm the address bar stays /sampleregister (no password in the URL).

Sample output (same HTML Tomcat prints at http://localhost:8080/basic-servlets/...):

request.getMethod() = POST
fullname = Rahul
roll = 101
email = rahul@college.edu
