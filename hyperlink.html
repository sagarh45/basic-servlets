Problem statement

Title: Open a servlet from a hyperlink (GET)

A normal is always GET. No form is needed.

What you must do:

  1. Click Execute. Tomcat runs doGet.
  2. Print the greeting stored in init().
  3. Write complete Java and complete web.xml mapped to /HyperLinkDemo.

Sample output (same HTML Tomcat prints at http://localhost:8080/basic-servlets/...):

Hello world! MY first Servlet Program...
